## Summary

- **Step 0.** The blog index and `sitemap-blog-en.xml` already matched: 25 posts each, live. Search Console's 26 predated the Incoterms merge. A new gate keeps them identical.
- **How-it-works vs `/export-process/`.** Owner decision: keep both, with separate keywords. They share 3% of their text.

- **Titles, metas and H1s.** Applied to all 135 URLs:
  - titles are ≤60 characters, front-load the primary keyword, include "India" or "Indian" (it was missing from 16 of 25 blog titles) and add a differentiator;
  - metas are 120–155 characters;
  - market pages are retargeted from the exporter's phrasing ("Export from India to X") to the buyer's ("Import from India to X").
- **Consolidation (owner decision).** Six product × country pages that duplicated a blog post's product and country now 301 to those posts.
- **Insight pass.** All 25 posts, in five sequential batches:
  - 101 marked insight sections, with at least 3 per post;
  - at least 4 question-shaped H2s per post;
  - 5 key takeaways each;
  - term coverage at least 84% (target 70%) against `research/term-sets.json`.
- **New gates in `npm run check`:**
  - `check-keywords.mjs`: unique primaries, title/meta/H1 rules, blog index = sitemap;
  - `check-blog-insight.mjs`: question H2s, insights, takeaways, term coverage, anchor-text variety (strict for every post).
- **Gap analysis.** `research/content-gaps.md` proposes 20 new posts, awaiting your approval. Their keyword rows are in `research/keyword-map-proposed.csv`.

## Errors found and fixed along the way

Each batch was verified independently, not on the agent's word:
- every derived figure (unit value = value ÷ quantity, shares, container ratios) was recomputed from the trade-data files;
- every number in the old source was diffed against the new page.

The cross-checks turned up problems outside the posts being edited:

- **China.** GACC Decree 280 replaced Decree 248 on 1 June 2026, per Australia's Department of Agriculture (IAN 2026-28). Five China pages still presented 248 as current. All are corrected, noting that 248 registrations remain valid.
- **Fruit container loads.** Several fruit pages claimed loads above a 40ft reefer's payload:
  - grapes 36–40 MT, now ~12–13 MT (APEDA season data: 36,888 t in about 2,930 containers);
  - banana 40–42 MT, now ~19–20 MT (1,400–1,540 × 13 kg cartons);
  - pomegranate 38–42 MT, now ~15–17 MT.
  - The 20ft row is now marked as not typical for these fruits.
- **Dehydrated onion loads.** They mixed powder and flakes figures. They are now form-dependent on the product page and both posts.
- **Transit-time metas.** Turmeric–UAE ("under a week") and chickpeas–UAE (3–6 days) contradicted their own posts. They now say 10–14 and 5–8 days.
- **Implied claims.** The approved title/meta proposals had already dropped phrasing that read as a certification or testing claim ("aflatoxin-tested", "GACC-registered packing", "FSVP-ready", "EU MRL-tested").
